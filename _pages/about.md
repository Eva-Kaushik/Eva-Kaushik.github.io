I am a PhD student in Data Science and Engineering at the Bredesen Center, a joint program of the University of Tennessee, Knoxville and Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL). I am a Graduate Research Assistant under the UT-ORII collaboration, working in ORNL's Sensors and Electronics Division with Dr. Kris Villez, and a GATE Fellow for 2026-27. My work on the security of learned traffic signal control is with Dr. Fnu Suya at the University of Tennessee and Dr. Jinghui Yuan and Dr. Wan Li at ORNL.

Before the PhD I spent three years in industry as a data scientist, first at DXC Technology and then on a generative AI program for Nestle, and I was the technical co-founder of the start-up Dexignare. I hold a B.Tech in Information Technology from Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University, Delhi, where I graduated in the top 3% of my class.

# Research Summary
My background is in statistical machine learning: Bayesian inference, high-dimensional modeling, causal inference and survival analysis, with applications in healthcare. My current research is on the security of machine learning systems after they are deployed. I study how learned controllers behave under attack when they run physical infrastructure such as traffic signals, and how the optimizations applied to a model for deployment, such as quantization, can change what it is safe to assume about it. Take a look at my [publications](/publications/).

# Current work
* **Adversarial attacks on learned traffic signal control.** How falsified vehicle detections can steer reinforcement-learning signal controllers, studied in simulation. A University of Tennessee and ORNL collaboration.
* **Security of AI systems under deployment optimizations.** What happens to a model's security guarantees when components such as quantization are added for efficient inference.
* **GridNervo.** Co-founder and technical lead of an early-stage venture developed through the technology commercialization course at the University of Tennessee.
